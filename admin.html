Extract Content from Google Docs & Sheets

Supports both Google Docs and Google Sheets. You can paste the full URL or just the ID.

Examples:

  • Google Doc URL: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ABC123.../edit
  • Google Sheet URL: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XYZ789.../edit
  • Just ID: 1ABC123... (will default to Google Doc)
  • Prefixed ID: sheet:1XYZ789... (explicitly specify as Sheet)
